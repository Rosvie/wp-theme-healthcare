Mission Statement

At Northfield Home Health Services, our mission is to deliver exceptional, cost-effective, and family-focused healthcare within the community. We ensure the well-being of individuals in our care.

Our Goal

  • Guarantee the provision of quality and comprehensive healthcare services to every patient through a coordinated plan of care from dedicated providers.
  • Uphold residents’ dignity, cultural sensitivity, and respect throughout service delivery.
  • Cultivate the professional and personal satisfaction of our employees by fostering a positive environment that encourages pride and professional growth.
  • Enhance community awareness of health-related issues.
